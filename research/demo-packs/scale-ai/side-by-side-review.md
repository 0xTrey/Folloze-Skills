# Scale AI Demo Pack Side-by-Side Review

Reviewed: 2026-07-20

## Pack Separation

| Experience | Motion | Buyer job | Story and proof | Primary interaction | Conversion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Production Systems | One-to-many campaign | Select an enterprise workflow whose AI system must perform in production | Scale GenAI Platform operating loop and Scale-published representative outcomes | Four-workflow production workbench | Map a production workflow |
| Mayo Clinic Reliable Care | One-to-one account page | Align clinical, AI, data, risk, and operations leaders on the next reliable-care workflow | Scale's public Mayo collaboration, eleven-minute result, and three public collaboration lanes | Prepare / Protect / Extend decision workbench | Plan a 90-day reliability review |
| Churn Prevention Webinar | Event promotion | Decide whether the July 28 session answers a role-specific retention question | Official event promise, agenda, speakers, date, time, and registration route | Retention / data science / customer operations learning path | Register on the official Scale Events page |

## Overlap Audit

- Hero language, section order, proof sets, interactions, and conversion jobs are different across all three pages.
- Shared Scale styling is limited to the brand system: black, white, restrained green, geometric typography, mono utility text, compact controls, and sparse dividers.
- The campaign does not name Mayo or reuse webinar content.
- The Mayo page does not present a cold-acquisition story; it treats the public collaboration as the starting point.
- The event page does not become a product overview; it stays anchored to a dated session and official registration.
- No private deal-note names, emails, technology observations, or internal research appear in buyer-facing copy.

## Final Recommendation

Keep the three pages as a pack. Together they demonstrate a useful funnel progression—enterprise demand, account-specific alignment, and event conversion—without turning one long page into three cosmetic variants.
