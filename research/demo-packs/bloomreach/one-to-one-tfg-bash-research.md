# Bloomreach One-To-One Research: TFG/Bash

Page: artifacts/bloomreach-demo-pack/bloomreach-one-to-one-tfg-bash.html

## Buyer Goal

Help Bash evaluate the next step after a successful Black Friday conversational-agent test: move guided shopping assistance earlier in the journey while keeping recommendations grounded in catalog, merchandising rules, and measurable shopper behavior.

## Account And Proof

- TFG implemented Loomi Conversational Agent on Bash, TFG's ecommerce platform. Source: https://www.bloomreach.com/en/case-studies/tfg-boosts-online-conversion-rate-with-bloomreach
- Bash ran an A/B test where Loomi Conversational Agent was shown to shoppers who engaged with at least three product pages. Source: same case study.
- Bloomreach reports +35.2% conversion rate, +39.8% revenue per visit, and -28.1% exit rate for shoppers who interacted with the agent during Black Friday. Source: same case study.
- The case study says TFG hoped/planned to expand the agent onto product listing pages under the Add to Cart button with pre-generated questions. Source: same case study.
- Loomi Conversational Agent is grounded in real-time catalog, personalization data, and merchandising rules; it includes VoC insights and multi-step reasoning. Source: https://www.bloomreach.com/en/products/loomi-conversational-agent
- June 24, 2026 enhancements include a conversational insights dashboard, advanced conversational engine, and connected conversational ecosystem. Source: https://www.bloomreach.com/en/news/2026/bloomreach-transforms-customer-conversation-into-ecommerce-insight-with-conversational-agent/

## Expansion Thesis

The first win was late-stage PDP guidance during Black Friday. The next move is earlier journey guidance on PLPs and connected commerce moments, with every answer grounded in Bash's catalog, rules, and real shopper intent.
