## Inputs

- Existing customer with six Creator licenses, unlimited boards, ABX, Personalization Engine, Analytics and Content Engine in its fictional package.
- MAP and CRM integrations are included. Current connections and mappings are unknown.
- Two teams want reusable partner pages and roundtable campaigns, with shared brand structure and audience-specific content.
- First application: one partner campaign. Assets and launch date will be agreed in the working session.
- Enablement: confirm creators, provide training, build the first page together and review engagement.
- Next actions: the marketing lead supplies brand assets; the CSM confirms access and training; the teams select the campaign. Dates and owner acceptance are unconfirmed.
- Private internal note: the platform needs broader use. Exclude that judgment and do not invent usage statistics.
- Training/example destinations and the primary CTA are supplied at run time. Missing destinations stay in internal review, not fake links.

## Show these decisions

1. Six included licenses stay separate from active users and configured integrations.
2. The argument is first successful use, without an invented expansion opportunity.
3. Two template families appear because the input calls for two. Google names, four families and package quantities are not defaults.
4. Each family makes standardization, adaptation and measurement easy to compare.
5. Unconfirmed owners/dates stay proposed; the primary CTA needs a real destination before release.
6. A separately authorized real run can save, read back, publish and verify.
