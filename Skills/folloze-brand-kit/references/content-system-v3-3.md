# Folloze Content System V3.3

Current Folloze messaging alignment. Reviewed 2026-10-27 against Tyler's October 26, 2026 Live update.

This file supersedes v3.2 for new marketing, sales, outbound, and agent-generated content. Older versions remain historical references. Product facts and customer proof still require the current capability and claims references.

## Lead Positioning

Lead with AI-driven ABM and define it in the same piece: your AI researches and writes; Folloze turns that work into campaigns for accounts across the funnel, with a governed experience that adapts by account, persona, and stage and captures engagement to guide the next move.

The promise is one-to-one impact at segment-level effort. Use "feels one-to-one" for impact. A one-to-one build can describe one named-account experience, such as a deal room.

Do not promise one-to-one production for every account. Avoid "1:1 at scale," "one-to-one at scale," "1:1 for every account," "unlimited," and "without limits."

Folloze helps teams target and convert key accounts. "Microsite" names an output, not Folloze's category. Describe Folloze as delivering campaigns and experiences, not as a landing page builder.

## What Changed

- AI-driven ABM becomes the lead positioning term, always paired with its definition.
- The experience exists to produce useful first-party engagement signal.
- One governed experience can adapt for multiple accounts, personas, and stages.
- Explain research, deployment, and learning in the marketer's order.
- Strengthen the Signal pillar and Signal-led value statement.
- Add Field marketing and CEO personas to the brief.
- Keep internal sales lenses and unverified proof out of public copy.

## Core Beliefs

1. Relevant account messaging improves the chance of conversion.
2. AI reduces the work of research and writing for teams that use it well.
3. Folloze works with the team's current tools and campaign approach.
4. Many segment, regional, product, and event audiences already contain named sets of accounts, even when the team does not call the program ABM.
5. Personalization earns attention. Engagement signal informs what marketing and sales do next.

Do not turn these positioning principles into guaranteed customer outcomes.

## The Marketer's Three Beats

| Beat | Responsibility | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research | The customer's AI and approved sources | Research and writing use CRM, call notes, intent data, and the web as permitted. Do not claim Folloze owns this input. |
| Deploy | Folloze | Turn that input into a governed campaign experience that adapts by account, persona, and stage through supported MCP, API, or platform workflows. |
| Learn | Folloze and the customer's stack | Show who engaged, with what, for how long, and from which channel; route supported engagement data to sales, MAP, CRM, and the next campaign. |

Use Build. Activate. Signal. for capability display copy. Research, Deploy, Learn describes the marketer's workflow. It does not replace the capability register.

## Engagement Intelligence

Lead with the decision: know which accounts are leaning in, what they read, and what to send next.

Then explain identity, depth, and openness:
- Identity connects the reader and account to engagement across supported channels.
- Depth shows asset consumption and time spent, beyond a page visit.
- Openness makes engagement available to connected sales and marketing workflows, warehouses, or CDPs where supported.

Keep content inside the experience when supported so engagement remains observable. Describe actual configuration and identity coverage accurately. Never imply every anonymous visitor is identified or every integration is enabled.

Include this signal beat whenever a piece explains how Folloze works. Do not lead with a count of identity sources.

Use "Claude and Folloze give every account the ABM treatment" as a use-case hook only with the definition and impact guardrails above.

## Competitive Framing

For internal preparation, focus on operating differences:
- Generated-page alternatives: contrast output volume with governed deployment, dynamic adaptation, engagement measurement, and distribution.
- MAP pages or a build-it-yourself workflow: explain how Folloze complements existing sends with account experiences and first-party engagement that informs the next send.
- 6sense and Demandbase: activate what they identify. Do not claim Folloze replaces them.

Verify current competitor behavior before making a named external comparison. Do not repeat categorical claims that another tool cannot measure, personalize, or govern without current evidence.

## Voice And Positioning Guardrails

- Your AI researches and writes. Folloze makes it a campaign and captures engagement.
- Never say AI builds the campaign on its own.
- Keep activation layer and internal agent names out of general external content.
- Avoid headless and open architecture in external copy. Describe the actual workflow.
- Do not put the customer's strategy on trial. The customer owns strategy and pipeline outcomes.
- Use agentic or autonomous only for specific behavior that is actually supported.
- Keep AI readiness and deal-size/account-count calculations as internal discovery lenses.
- Do not use em dashes or generic SaaS hype.
- Start visual assets with one headline. Do not stack an eyebrow, headline, and explanatory subtitle.
- Use 1 through 9 in visual numbering, without leading zeros.
