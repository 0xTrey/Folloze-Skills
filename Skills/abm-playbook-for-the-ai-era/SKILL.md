# ABM Playbook for the AI Era

Create a buyer-facing playbook that lets the account model its own pipeline target, choose where 1:1 pays, and agree on what to test. Use the Folloze-branded six-chapter [starter](assets/starter/page.html). Deliver a standalone review, a Folloze HTML fragment, and an internal evidence ledger.

## Start with the deal

- Resolve the account, opportunity, audience, and intended next decision. Use the user's materials first. When authorized and relevant, corroborate with current meeting notes, CRM, account email, and prior customer records. Date the evidence and distinguish historical use, current use, and proposed options. Use older records as leads to check.
- Record the source, date, and status of each customer-specific claim, planning input, logo, and example. Keep that ledger with the account's private working files. Use verified facts in the copy, label options and scenarios, and leave unknown calculator inputs blank. Keep private notes, contact data, pricing discussions, and credentials out of the page.
- Read [the account brief and QA guide](references/account-build.md). Copy the fictional example brief into the deal's authorized repo, replace its values, and add approved assets as needed. Use the builder for every first draft so the navigation, calculator, print behavior, and layout are inherited.

## Shape the six chapters

1. **Overview.** Give one headline and one sentence on what the buyer can decide. Send visitors straight to the math.
2. **Math.** Ask how many accounts it takes to create the target pipeline. Let visitors edit pipeline target, average opportunity size, engagement rate, and engaged-to-opportunity rate. Divide the target by deal size and both rates, then round up required coverage. Keep modeled closed-won separate. Label benchmark defaults with their source and scope when verified. Historical benchmarks and illustrative values do not describe the buyer's actual performance.
3. **Coverage.** Use 1:few for scale. Move to 1:1 when an opportunity is open, its size justifies the build, or multiple stakeholders are engaging. Adapt cohorts and signals to the deal.
4. **Funnel.** Match examples to acquisition, buying-group education, and conversion or expansion. A deal room can become the account hub after close. When a real event example is available, show how one link serves before, during, and after the event. Use linked thumbnails and concise direct buttons. Offer copy-and-paste instructions for personalized examples that need a clean browser session. Omit missing examples.
5. **Stack + Workflow.** Connect three beats. The account's AI researches and drafts, Folloze deploys and personalizes, and engagement flows to sellers and the next campaign. Show the seller action and where it lands when verified. Keep tool chips short. Label unconfirmed paid, nurture, or seller-email channels as options or omit them.
6. **Build + Buy.** Name what the account owns, what Folloze supplies, and the smallest pilot with a measurable decision. Agree on launch cohort, baseline, signals, seller follow-up, and the expansion threshold. Include a setup offer only when scope and pricing are approved.

Keep the Folloze visual identity by default. Use approved marks, a consistent left-aligned heading system, and no numbered section titles or eyebrow-headline-dek stacks. Use the built-in motion with reduced-motion support. Route separately if the user specifically requests another builder.

## Review and release

- Share the local review with the evidence gaps and a clear next decision. Verify the calculator, linked examples, clipboard behavior when configured, keyboard and mobile navigation, and PDF output.
- Before accessing Folloze, apply the available instance-routing guidance. Keep any existing board on its verified instance. Use current section guidance and analytics standards for the actual target. A prior account's publication and board ID confer no authority for this build.
- Save or publish only within the user's current authorization. Read back the saved fragment, then check the actual visitor view and interaction tracking if published. Report local review, saved draft, publication, visitor checks, and delivery separately.
